Watch for alcohol poisoning

This much alcohol, this fast, can be dangerous. Get help straight away if you or a friend shows any of these:

  • Confused, or very hard to wake
  • Vomiting while drowsy
  • Slow or irregular breathing
  • Cold, pale or bluish skin
  • A seizure

Never leave someone to sleep it off alone. Lie them on their side so they can't choke.

              How Dry Season works

              You tell Dry Season what you drink and when. It estimates how much alcohol is in your blood over time and counts down to when your body should be clear.

              Logging your night

              • Add a drink when you open one. If nothing is going yet, choose when you started: just now, a little while ago, or right after your last round.
              • Rounds are stretches of drinking. Drinks in a round are spread evenly from its start to its end, so it doesn't matter exactly when each one went down.
              • Got a few on hand? Add them all with Just now and choose Open 1, save the rest for later. Your stash waits on the main screen. Tap Open next each time you crack one.
              • Forgot to log? No problem. When you come back after a while, the app asks how many you've opened since and spreads them across the gap.
              • Had drinks before opening the app? Use Log earlier drinks to set a round with the times you drank.
              • Got a time wrong? Tap Adjust times on any round to move its start or finish by 15 minutes.
              • Tap Finished drinking when you're done for the night.

              Log without opening the app

              On Android, long press the Dry Season icon and tap One more. It logs the next drink from your stash, or another of your last drink, in a couple of seconds.

              Setting it up on iPhone

              iPhones don't show shortcuts on web app icons, but the Shortcuts app can do the same job.

              • Open Shortcuts, tap +, add the Open URLs action and paste this app's address with ?action=one at the end.
              • Name it One more. You can then run it from a home screen icon, by asking Siri, with Back Tap (Settings, Accessibility, Touch, Back Tap), or from the Action Button on newer iPhones.

              Reading the screen

              • Clear by is when your blood alcohol should reach zero. The countdown runs live.
              • The status pill tells you whether you're likely under or over your legal limit right now.
              • The night scene shows your evening as hills: higher hills mean more alcohol. The glass empties as you sober up.
              • The risk scale shows where you are today, from impaired to danger, with a pacing nudge when you're drinking fast.
              • Driving soon? Set when you need to drive and you'll see whether you're likely to be clear, with an hour to spare.

              What it can't know

              Dry Season uses a well known formula with your weight, body type and how fast you clear alcohol. Real bodies vary with food, sleep, health and medication, so every number is an estimate.

              Never use this to decide whether you can drive. If you're unsure, don't. Get a lift, take a cab, or stay over.
