Watch for alcohol poisoning

This much alcohol, this fast, can be dangerous. Get help straight away if you or a friend shows any of these:

  • Confused, or very hard to wake
  • Vomiting while drowsy
  • Slow or irregular breathing
  • Cold, pale or bluish skin
  • A seizure

Never leave someone to sleep it off alone. Lie them on their side so they can't choke.

        How fast your body clears alcohol
