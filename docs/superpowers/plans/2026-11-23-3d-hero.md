# 3D Hero Implementation Plan

**Goal:** Bring the approved 3D prototype (https://claude.ai/artifact/NBbskazWacMjEEar7YrRrp) into the app's hero: a refracting glass, a cold bottle that pours, and a sunset with depth that tilts with the phone.

**Architecture:** A new `scene3d.js` (`window.DryScene`) owns the three.js scene. Its pure helpers (glass level, palette from CSS tokens, camera framing) are tested in Node. three.js r149 is saved in `vendor/` so the app stays offline. The page hands the scene the night each second; the scene draws only when something moves. Without WebGL the current flat glass and SVG horizon stay exactly as they are.

## Decisions

1. **Layout follows the prototype.** The readout, time and stats sit on top; the bottle and glass stand below them on the ground. The old SVG glass is hidden when 3D runs.
2. **Your night is the landscape.** The BAC horizon from 5.0 becomes the nearest range of hills in the 3D scene, drawn from `DryHorizon.paths` onto a texture: ridge glow, round bands and labels, the leftover line, the now dot and the moon. The SVG horizon stays as the fallback.
3. **The glass empties as you sober up.** Level = 0.86 × (time left to clear ÷ the night's length), so it is full just after you start and empty when you are clear.
4. **Pour once per night.** When the night goes from empty to having a drink (live or late), the bottle pours. Later drinks raise the level smoothly. Removing every round returns the sealed bottle.
5. **Empty state copy:** "Tonight", "Clear", "Nothing on the tab yet. Your first pour starts the clock." Stats are hidden until there is something on the tab.
6. **Colours come from the app's tokens** (`--sky-top`, `--sky-mid`, `--sky-low`, `--horizon`, `--ground`, `--amber`, `--ink`) and update when the theme changes, so light and dark both match. The ground is exactly `--ground`, so the hero meets the page seamlessly.
7. **Tilt** follows the phone's motion (iOS asks once, on the first tap of the hero), or a horizontal drag. No idle sway, to save battery.
8. **Battery:** draw only while tilting, pouring, the level is changing or bubbles are rising (bubbles at about 20 frames a second), and stop when the hero is off screen or the app is hidden.
9. **Reduce Motion:** no tilt, no bubbles, and the pour jumps straight to the result.
10. **The hand off orb** lands on the BAC peak in the 3D scene.

## Tasks

1. `scene3d.js` pure helpers with tests: `glassLevel`, `palette`, `frame`.
2. Vendor three.js, add it and `scene3d.js` to the offline list, with the offline test extended to `vendor/`.
3. The scene: backdrop, glass, bottle, condensation, lime, bubbles, BAC hills texture, pour, tilt, render on demand, theme updates.
4. Hero integration: canvas, layout, empty state copy, level and pour wiring, orb target, fallback.
5. Browser check: the 3D hero appears, the empty state reads "Clear", the first round pours and the scene ends in its night state.
6. Release 5.1.0 with notes and README.
